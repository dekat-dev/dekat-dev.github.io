Schedules

Dock and undock automatically at the times and days you set, no tap needed.

Strict Mode

Stops Dock being uninstalled or switched off while you're docked.

Emergency Undock

Your time, at a glance

See how long you've stayed docked today and on average, day by day.

Calm themes

Each theme pairs a palette with its own animated scene: koi, a lava lamp, a record, a setting sun.

Backup, if you want it

Optionally keep your setup in your own Google Drive, so a new phone picks up where you left off.

Private by design

Dock needs no account. Your blocked apps, history and schedules stay on your phone, or in your own Google Drive if you turn on backup. Usage stats are anonymous and can be turned off. Read the privacy policy.
